Since 1989

Thirty-five years of one client at a time

Kip Jawish founded IN-FIT in 1989 with a simple idea: personal training should actually be personal. Three and a half decades later, the studio is newly renovated, the approach has not changed, and Frederick keeps voting it the best in town.

Small. Private. Never intimidating. That is the whole point.

Session one, filmed by Perkins Production

Now imagine it filmed

The studio was just renovated. The website should look like it.

These dark panels are placeholders, because the new space has never been properly shot. The finished site opens with real film and photography made inside IN-FIT by Perkins Production: chalk in the air over the turf, a trainer coaching through a final rep, the quiet before a 6am session.

A studio this private wins on trust. Nothing builds it faster than seeing the real room and the real coaching before you ever walk in.

Twelve straight years of Best of Frederick deserves a site that looks the part.
