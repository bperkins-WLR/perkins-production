The story

One family, two towns, forty-some years of full plates

Mountain Gate opened in Thurmont in 1979 and has stayed in the family ever since, growing to a second location in Waynesboro, Pennsylvania.

Come hungry, stay a while: after dinner there are on-site country gift shops to wander before you get back on the road.

Groups welcome

The restaurant tour buses plan their route around

Sitting at 133 Frederick Rd on the way to Catoctin Mountain and Camp David, Mountain Gate has been a motorcoach destination for decades. A buffet built for a busload says everything about the kitchen.

Every photo panel on this page is an empty seat at the table, on purpose. No stock food photos, ever: your buffet line, your bakers, your dining room, photographed and filmed at the restaurant by Perkins Production.
