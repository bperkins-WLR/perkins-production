What's in the building

Eight ways to lose track of time

Sports cardsCases of cards spanning eras, waiting for the collector who knows what they're looking at.
Antique clocksMantel, wall, and shelf clocks from dealers who know their movements.
Vintage clothingRacks of wearable history, one size run and one owner at a time.
QuiltsHand-pieced and machine-stitched, the kind that get passed down for a reason.
GlasswareShelves that catch the light: patterns, colors, and complete sets still together.
PrimitivesFarmhouse pieces with the wear left honest, straight out of the countryside.
Mid-Century ModernClean lines and warm teak for the collectors chasing the 20th century.
Paper ephemeraPostcards, advertisements, and printed odds and ends that survived on purpose.

On the finished site, each category links to a browsable dealer directory, so more than 120 dealers finally get a page of their own.

Since 1988

A destination, not a detour

Emmitsburg Antique Mall has anchored 1 Chesapeake Ave for nearly four decades: one building, thousands of stories, and a steady stream of day-trippers who plan their whole Saturday around it.

Make a day of it

Worth the drive from anywhere

Minutes from Catoctin Mountain and an easy run down from Gettysburg, the mall is the stop that turns a scenic drive into a full day out. Come for the hunt, stay for the penny candy and a bottled soda on the way out the door.

  • Open every day, 10am to 5pm, except major holidays
  • Dog-friendly, so the whole family browses together
  • Wheelchair accessible throughout the building

Now imagine it filmed

The finished site opens with your aisles on camera

These placeholder panels are standing in for the real thing: cinematic film and photography shot inside your building by Perkins Production. Sunlight through the front windows, a dealer arranging a case, the candy counter at ten in the morning.

A store this size can't fit in a phone screen, but the feeling of walking in can. That is what we film, and that is what makes a day-tripper choose Emmitsburg.
