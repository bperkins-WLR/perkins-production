Since 1978

A trade learned by hand, kept by hand

Byron L. Hawkins apprenticed in the trade in the early 1970s, founded Artistic Masonry in 1978, and incorporated the company in 1997. Nearly five decades later he is still the owner, the president, and the standard.

The techniques are the ones the trade was built on: stone shaped with a chisel, joints finished in lime-based mortar, the same methods that raised the historic buildings this company is trusted to restore.
