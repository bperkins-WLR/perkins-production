The story

A family company, thirty years on

Overlook Fence Company has been family owned since 1992. Three decades in, the trucks still roll out of Frederick every weekday morning, and the same standard applies to every job: posts set straight, lines run true, gates that swing right the first time.

That is what more than thirty years in fencing looks like. Not a slogan, just a habit the family never broke.
