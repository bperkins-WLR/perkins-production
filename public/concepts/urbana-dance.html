Urbana is one of the fastest-growing corners of Frederick County, and its dancers grow up here: from a first creative-movement class at two and a half to pointe shoes and competition stages.

Eight disciplines, one studio

Find their class

BalletThe foundation of it all: posture, musicality, and grace at the barre.
PointeThe milestone years of ballet, for dancers ready to rise.
TapRhythm you can hear: shuffles, time steps, and speed.
JazzSharp, stylized, and full of performance energy.
Hip HopGroove, freestyle, and choreography set to today's music.
ContemporaryWhere technique meets expression and storytelling.
ModernGrounded, powerful movement beyond the classical line.
TumblingStrength, flexibility, and acro skills that carry across styles.

Classes for ages 2.5 and up. On the finished site, every class links straight to its day, time, and open spots.

Two ways to dance here

Recreational or competitive, every dancer has a lane

Recreational

One great class a week, a recital to work toward, and a lifelong love of dance. No pressure, all joy.

Competitive

For dancers who want more: focused training and the thrill of taking the stage against the region's best.

The neighborhood studio

Where Urbana's dancers grow up

On Worthington Boulevard, in the heart of the Villages of Urbana, this studio is where southern Frederick County's kids learn what practice, patience, and a stage can do.

Eight disciplines under one roof means a dancer never has to leave home to grow: the two-and-a-half-year-old in her first ballet class and the competitive team training for the season share the same hallway, and the same teachers cheering them on.
