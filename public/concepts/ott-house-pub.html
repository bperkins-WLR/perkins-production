The patch wall

Every class leaves a piece of home on the wall

Students from the National Fire Academy have been pinning their department patches to these walls for years. Fire companies from every corner of the country, shoulder to shoulder, floor to ceiling.

It means firefighters across America already know the Ott House by name. A proper website lets them find you before their class even arrives in Emmitsburg.

Since 1970

Bud opened the doors. The family never left.

Bernard "Bud" Ott opened the pub in 1970, and 55 years later it is still the family's place: four Ott siblings run the day-to-day, the Mount St. Mary's crowd keeps coming back long after graduation, and there's live music on a regular basis.

These panels are placeholders for the real thing: warm, cinematic film and photography shot inside the Ott House by Perkins Production. The patch wall in detail, wings coming out of the kitchen, a band setting up, the glow of Main Street through the front window.

Fifty-five years built this room's atmosphere. Ten seconds of honest footage puts it on every phone in Emmitsburg, at the Mount, and in every firehouse that's ever pinned a patch here.
