The homestead

Some farms grow one thing. This family grows a way of life.

Sycamore Spring is a small family homestead in Frederick built around an old idea: that a piece of land, tended by hand, can feed the people who live on it and the neighbors who visit it.

Goats, poultry, rabbits, bees, and cattle share the place with a food forest and gardens of sixty-some crops. And the family teaches what it practices, through permaculture design and food-forest education, so the simpler way of life travels home with you.
