01 Where the current site falls short

The website works against the gym

I went through every page and link on sportsinnfitness.com. The leagues are clearly well run; the website just makes them harder to find and join.

✦Every league schedule and standing is a hand-uploaded PDF, nearly unreadable on a phone.
✦The "Event Sign Up" and "Roster" pages are empty placeholder images with no forms behind them.
✦Joining a league means emailing "ADD ME" to the front desk, then paying in person.
✦No prices appear anywhere on the site, so every serious question becomes a phone call.
✦Every update means editing and re-uploading files by hand, which is how links end up broken.
02 What you get

Three pieces, one system

See the mockup

A modern website

Built for phones, using your own logos and photos: home, amenities, sand volleyball, a join-a-league signup form, training, pricing, contact, and a My Team area with a league calendar, game rescheduling, and team chat.

Square checkout

Online signup and payment

Players pick a division, register a team or join as a free agent, e-sign the waiver, and pay the league fee online. The money lands directly in your Square account.

One login

A staff admin page

Type in the team names and the site builds the whole season: matchups, courts 1 and 2, time slots, and which team works each game, all balanced automatically. Reffing teams enter scores courtside and standings recalculate on the spot; your staff just approve, okay reschedule swaps, see who has signed up and paid, and flip a "courts closed tonight" banner when it storms. When the season ends, the top 6 playoff bracket builds itself from the standings.

04 The hours it hands back

What stops being your job

Season schedulingHours per league by hand, now seconds. Type the team names and every matchup, court, time slot, and work-team shift generates conflict-free, playoff bracket included.
Weekly updatesThe PDF grind disappears. Instead of exporting and uploading schedule and standings files for six leagues all summer, work teams enter scores courtside and everything updates itself. Staff just approve.
RegistrationTwo minutes online instead of a phone call chain. Roster, ratings, waiver, and Square payment in one flow, and the front desk stops fielding "what does it cost" calls because prices are on the site.
Rule policingRatings check themselves. The coed limits (one A-level male, no AA) are enforced at signup instead of by eye against the rule sheet.
RainoutsOne tap, not a phone tree. A banner on every page plus the email list replaces calling captains one by one.
