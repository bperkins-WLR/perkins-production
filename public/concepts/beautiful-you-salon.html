The story

From a jewelry store to a jewel of Water Street

Owner Sandy Willis built her salon in Emmitsburg, where it was known as Emmitsburg Day Spa. In October 2020 she moved it into the renovated former Browns' Jewelry Store in downtown Thurmont, and the town celebrated with a ribbon cutting.

It stayed a family affair: Sandy's mother Yvonne Willis greets you at the front desk, and a team of nine carries the work forward every day.
