Roughly 49 seasons of trees

Almost half a century of families finding their tree here

Mayne's has been the choose-and-cut tradition in Buckeystown for going on 49 seasons. Walk the field, pick the one, cut it yourself, and ride the hayride back. Custom wreaths, roping, and door decorations are made right at the farm, and farm tours are available by appointment.
