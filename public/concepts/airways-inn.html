The view

Dinner with a view of the runway

Airways Inn has been serving at Frederick Municipal Airport since 1996, and the dining room at 310 Aviation Way looks straight out onto the runway. Kids press their noses to the glass, pilots taxi past on their way to lunch, and everybody else just orders another coffee and watches.

It is a casual family spot and a laid-back business lunch all at once: the kind of place you bring visitors when you want Frederick to show off a little.
